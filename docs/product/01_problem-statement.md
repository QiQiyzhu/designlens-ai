# DesignLens — Product one-pager

Status: **PENDING REAL USER RESEARCH**. Updated 2026-09-10. This is a product hypothesis and functioning portfolio MVP, not a validated business.

## Product one-liner
DesignLens helps a product owner carry a decision from its original evidence through an opportunity, architecture choice, versioned workflow, evaluation and experiment protocol.

## Problem hypothesis
A summary is easy to produce; an auditable decision is harder. When feedback lives in files, requirements in documents and experiment results in an analytics tool, the owner must manually maintain the link between a claim and its evidence, distinguish model advice from their decision, and remember which prompt produced a result. We have not established the frequency or cost of this problem with target users.

## Job to be done
When I review a proposed AI feature, I want to inspect the evidence, alternatives, failure cases and test results together, so I can decide whether to build, revise or stop it without treating fluent text as proof.

## Initial wedge
An individual PM conducting a small qualitative pilot, especially for a complex game onboarding experience. ARC//SHIFT is the planned first real research case. Its existing game code is product context, not evidence that a player experienced a problem.

## Value hypothesis and falsification
Value would be demonstrated if a PM can explain and reconstruct a decision using source links with fewer missing assumptions than their current tools. We stop or pivot if interviews show that provenance is already effortless, decisions seldom require evaluation, or the structured workflow introduces more work than it removes. A faster document draft alone does not validate this product.

## MVP promise
Import evidence; review extractive observations; confirm opportunities; compare AI and non-AI approaches; execute a versioned evidence workflow; inspect actual rule checks; create a pre-registered experiment. Every default example is DEMO/SYNTHETIC. No autonomous prioritization, business success claims or automatic actions in ARC//SHIFT.

## Evidence ledger
Public competitor documents support the market landscape. Automated tests support software behavior. Target-user need, willingness to switch, usable decision time and business impact remain unknown.
