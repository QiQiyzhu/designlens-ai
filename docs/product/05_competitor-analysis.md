## Why not ChatGPT + Notion + Amplitude?
That stack may be the better choice. It already supports writing, organizing and measurement. DesignLens is a narrow hypothesis: maintaining a typed chain, Evidence → Opportunity → Feasibility → versioned Workflow → Evaluation → Experiment, with explicit human gates, can make the rationale and uncertainty easier to inspect. This is workflow packaging, not a claim that competitors cannot do it.

Test the difference through the same redacted decision task in both workflows. Measure missing evidence, reconstruction success, correction effort and time; record setup cost. If the existing stack performs equally with less friction, prefer an export template or integration product rather than another workspace.

## Research cautions
No numerical price comparison is fixed into the strategy: ChatPRD's public pricing and older team help text differed during this review; Notion pricing was localized. Recheck the live checkout before a purchasing decision. No hands-on accounts or customer interviews were used. We have not established willingness to switch, demand size or a durable moat.
