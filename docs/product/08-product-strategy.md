# Product strategy and scope control

**PENDING REAL USER RESEARCH**. This strategy is an explicit bet, not product-market fit.

The wedge is a small decision packet: one question, consented evidence, an accepted opportunity, an architecture comparison, a tested workflow and an experiment protocol. Optimize for reviewability rather than generation volume. A useful result may be “do not build this” or “use clearer copy, not AI”.

The north star candidate is Weekly Validated Product Decisions, with a strict definition in [metrics](11-metrics.md). An experiment draft is not a validated decision. Until real data exists the product reports zero verified business outcomes and labels test activity as DEMO/SYNTHETIC.

Build locally first to make source inspection and test reproduction cheap. The initial API is single-workspace with SQLite. No claim of enterprise access control, shared editing, production data compliance or scale readiness. Public demos use synthetic data; private raw research stays out of Git and static deployment assets.

## Make / integrate / defer
Make the evidence-ID relationships, human gates, feasibility comparison and reproducible evaluation report. Integrate through exports now. Defer hosted OAuth connectors, arbitrary MCP execution, autonomous Agents, transcription, real-time collaboration and a general workflow automation marketplace. Existing products already do those better; reproducing them would dilute the product case.

## Risks and kill criteria
If the six-step chain is excessive for most decisions, shrink to a decision review checklist. If users only want synthesis, assess an integration with Dovetail or Notion. If evidence review does not reduce missed assumptions, do not market “trust” on appearance alone. Validate migration/setup cost and whether the opportunity owner and reviewer both benefit.

Potential business model is a workspace subscription or an existing-tool extension. No revenue forecasts or willingness-to-pay claims are warranted. Recruitment, interview artifacts and a comparison against a manual baseline are more valuable next steps than another feature page.
