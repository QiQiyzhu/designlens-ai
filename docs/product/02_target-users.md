# Target users and recruitment hypotheses

**PENDING REAL USER RESEARCH**. These are proposed recruitment segments, not research personas.

| Segment | Trigger / current workaround hypothesis | Need to validate | Initial scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| Individual or junior PM | Has several feedback files and must justify an AI feature | Does a decision actually get lost between tools? Who reviews it? | Primary |
| Small game team product owner | New players struggle with system comprehension | Which tasks fail, and can UI/rules solve them? | First case recruitment |
| Researcher / designer | Hands findings to a product owner | Does evidence survive prioritization and implementation? | Secondary reviewer |
| AI engineer / data analyst | Asked to judge prompt quality | What evaluation contract would make the request actionable? | Advisory role |
| Enterprise research operations | Many teams, permissions and continuous imports | Significant integration/compliance needs exceed the MVP | Deferred |

Recruit PMs who made at least one product decision in the past month and can show a redacted artifact. Include users of existing tools and people who do not want AI. Do not recruit only friends who praise the prototype. For ARC//SHIFT recruit 8–12 adult new players with mixed action roguelite familiarity; record gaming experience without inferring it from age or gender.

The buyer and user may differ. We will ask who controls tool purchase and data access, but have no pricing or willingness-to-pay finding. A student portfolio user is useful for interface testing but not proof of B2B demand.

Exclude research containing confidential employment data, identifiable third-party transcripts without consent, or minors from the initial study. Collect a pseudonymous participant ID, segment, consent state and session date; keep contact information separately and locally. A participant can withdraw their own session before analysis is frozen.

Success for the primary user is a defensible decision, including a decision not to use AI. It is not the number of generated insights or completed workflow nodes.
