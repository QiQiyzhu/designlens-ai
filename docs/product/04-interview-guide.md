# Interview guide

**PENDING REAL USER RESEARCH**. Moderator script, not a transcript.

## Opening (2 minutes)
“We are studying how decisions are made, not testing your ability. There are no right answers. You may skip any question or stop. May I take pseudonymous notes? Recording is optional and requires separate consent.” State retention and withdrawal rules before proceeding.

## Recent decision walkthrough (12–15 minutes)
Ask about the last real decision, not a general opinion: “What triggered it? Show the earliest evidence you can share. What did you do next? Who disagreed? Which artifact helped resolve that? What did you ship or decide not to ship?” Follow with “How did you know the problem was solved?”

Probe provenance without pitching the solution: “Can you find the source of that sentence? What information is missing? Was the uncertainty visible to the reviewer?” Ask for a counterexample where the existing tools worked well.

## AI decision probe (5 minutes)
“Was AI considered? What alternative did you compare? What happened when the output was wrong? Was there a latency or budget constraint? Who could override it?” Do not ask “Would an AI copilot be useful?”

## Prototype tasks (separate session, 10 minutes)
Use an explicitly synthetic task packet. Ask the participant to locate the evidence for one observation, reject an unsupported claim, record an opportunity decision, choose an architecture and interpret a failed evaluation. Observe assistance, completion and errors; do not teach the happy path first.

## Closing
“What would prevent you from using this? What does your current process do better? What should I have asked?” Request permission for a follow-up. Do not equate polite enthusiasm with adoption intent or purchase intent.

## Coding discipline
Record timestamped action, quote, moderator intervention and interpretation separately. Use “not observed” rather than guessing intent. The ARC//SHIFT 5–10 minute post-play script and blank observation/survey forms are in docs/research.
